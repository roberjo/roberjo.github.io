01 · About

Enterprise cloud architecture, applied to automation and AI

My background is designing and running enterprise systems on AWS: multi-account organizations, serverless and event-driven platforms, and the infrastructure as code, security controls, and observability that keep them healthy.

Lately I point that discipline at a different problem: getting real leverage out of LLMs. That means agents that turn vulnerability tickets into reviewed pull requests, tooling that teaches AI coding agents to use the tools your team already relies on, and modern engineering wrapped around systems most people would rather not touch, like mainframe COBOL batch.

  • Ship it tested. CI, golden files, and end-to-end tests are part of the design, not an afterthought.
  • Make it visible. If nobody can see into a system, it isn't done.
  • Spend deliberately. Many of my projects run entirely on free tiers, by design.
